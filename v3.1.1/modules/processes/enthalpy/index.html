    Module enthalpy

    Warning: this rather complex module was not much tested so far, use it with care!

    Description:

    This IGM module models the ice enthalpy, which permits to jointly model the ice temperature, as well as the water content created when the temperature hits the pressure melting points, and therefore energy conservation, which is not the case when modelling the sole temperature variable. The model is described in (Aschwanden and al, JOG, 2012). Check at the IGM technical paper for further details [1].

    [1] Concepts and capabilities of the Instructed Glacier Model 3.X.X, Jouvet and al.

    The enthalpy module builds upon the iceflow module. To ensure proper functionality, follow these requirements:

    • Activate the vertical_iceflow module to provide the vertical velocity.
    • Set params.dim_arrhenius = 3.
    • Set params.new_friction_param = true.
    • Ensure sufficient retraining by setting retrain_iceflow_emulator_freq = 1. Optionally, set retrain_iceflow_emulator_nbit to a value greater than 1 for improved performance.

    Contributors: G. Jouvet

    This implementation is largely inspired from the one implemented in PISM. Other references that have helped are (Kleiner and al, TC, 2015) and (Wang and al, 2020).

    Config Structure

    enthalpy:  
  numerics: 
    Nz: 10
    vert_spacing: 4.0
  solver:
    allow_basal_refreezing: True
    correct_w_for_melt: True
    override_basal_at_pmp: False
  thermal:
    T_min: 243.15
    T_pmp_ref: 273.15
    T_ref: 223.15
    c_ice: 2009.0
    L_ice: 3.34e+5
    k_ice: 2.1
    K_ratio: 1e-1
    basal_heat_flux_ref: 0.065
    beta: 7.9e-8
  surface:
    T_offset: 0.0
  arrhenius:
    A_cold: 3.985e-13
    A_warm: 1.916e+3
    Q_cold: 60000.0
    Q_warm: 139000.0
    T_threshold: 263.15
    omega_coef: 181.25
    omega_max: 0.01
    R: 8.314
  till:
    friction:
      phi: 30.0
      phi_min: 15.0
      phi_max: 45.0
      bed_min: null
      bed_max: null
      tauc_min: 1.0e+5
      tauc_max: 1.0e+10
      tauc_ice_free: 1.0e+6
      u_ref: 100.0
    hydro:
      h_water_till_max: 2.0
      N_ref: 1000.0
      e_ref: 0.69
      C_c: 0.12
      delta: 0.02
      drainage_rate: 0.001
  drainage:
    water_density: 1000.0
    drain_ice_column: True
    omega_target: 0.01
    omega_threshold_1: 0.01
    omega_threshold_2: 0.02
    omega_threshold_3: 0.03

    Parameters

    Name Type Units Description Default Value
    numerics.Nz integer \( dimless \) Number of vertical layers for the enthalpy discretization. 10
    numerics.vert_spacing float \( dimless \) Parameter controlling the vertical spacing; 1.0 means uniform, larger values concentrate points near the bed. 4.0
    solver.allow_basal_refreezing boolean \( dimless \) Allow negative basal melt rates (refreezing). True
    solver.correct_w_for_melt boolean \( dimless \) Correct vertical velocity for basal melting. True
    solver.override_basal_at_pmp boolean \( dimless \) Force basal enthalpy to be at pressure-melting point. False
    thermal.T_min float \( K \) Minimum temperature bound for enthalpy solver. 243.15
    thermal.T_pmp_ref float \( K \) Reference pressure melting point temperature. 273.15
    thermal.T_ref float \( K \) Reference temperature for enthalpy calculations. 223.15
    thermal.c_ice float \( J kg^{-1} K^{-1} \) Specific heat capacity of ice. 2009.0
    thermal.L_ice float \( J kg^{-1} \) Latent heat of fusion for ice. 334000.0
    thermal.k_ice float \( W m^{-1} K^{-1} \) Thermal conductivity of ice. 2.1
    thermal.K_ratio float \( dimless \) Ratio of temperate to cold ice thermal diffusivity. 1e-1
    thermal.basal_heat_flux_ref float \( W m^{-2} \) Reference geothermal heat flux (used when not specified in state). 0.065
    thermal.beta float \( K Pa^{-1} \) Clausius-Clapeyron constant. 7.9e-08
    surface.T_offset float \( °C \) Temperature offset for surface conditions. 0.0
    arrhenius.A_cold float \( Pa^{-n} s^{-1} \) Pre-exponential factor for cold ice. 3.985e-13
    arrhenius.A_warm float \( Pa^{-n} s^{-1} \) Pre-exponential factor for warm ice. 1916.0
    arrhenius.Q_cold float \( J mol^{-1} \) Activation energy for cold ice. 60000.0
    arrhenius.Q_warm float \( J mol^{-1} \) Activation energy for warm ice. 139000.0
    arrhenius.T_threshold float \( K \) Temperature threshold between cold and warm regimes. 263.15
    arrhenius.omega_coef float \( dimless \) Water content enhancement coefficient. 181.25
    arrhenius.omega_max float \( dimless \) Maximum water content for enhancement. 0.01
    arrhenius.R float \( J mol^{-1} K^{-1} \) Universal gas constant. 8.314
    till.friction.phi float \( ° \) Till friction angle (uniform case). 30.0
    till.friction.phi_min float \( ° \) Friction angle at bed_min. 15.0
    till.friction.phi_max float \( ° \) Friction angle at bed_max. 45.0
    till.friction.bed_min float \( m \) Minimum bed elevation for interpolation. None
    till.friction.bed_max float \( m \) Maximum bed elevation for interpolation. None
    till.friction.tauc_min float \( Pa \) Minimum yield stress. 100000.0
    till.friction.tauc_max float \( Pa \) Maximum yield stress. 10000000000.0
    till.friction.tauc_ice_free float \( Pa \) Yield stress for ice-free areas. 1000000.0
    till.friction.u_ref float \( m yr^{-1} \) Reference sliding velocity. 100.0
    till.hydro.h_water_till_max float \( m \) Maximum till water layer thickness. 2.0
    till.hydro.N_ref float \( Pa \) Reference effective pressure. 1000.0
    till.hydro.e_ref float \( dimless \) Reference void ratio. 0.69
    till.hydro.C_c float \( dimless \) Till compressibility coefficient. 0.12
    till.hydro.delta float \( dimless \) Minimum effective pressure fraction. 0.02
    till.hydro.drainage_rate float \( m yr^{-1} \) Till drainage rate. 0.001
    drainage.water_density float \( kg m^{-3} \) Water density constant. 1000.0
    drainage.drain_ice_column boolean \( dimless \) Enable water drainage through ice column. True
    drainage.omega_target float \( dimless \) Target water content after drainage. 0.01
    drainage.omega_threshold_1 float \( dimless \) First water content threshold. 0.01
    drainage.omega_threshold_2 float \( dimless \) Second water content threshold. 0.02
    drainage.omega_threshold_3 float \( dimless \) Third water content threshold. 0.03
